Overview of Godrej-Golf-Links

ATS Kingston Heath is a well-conceptualized residential township developed by Godrej Properties in Sector 10, Greater Noida West. Designed around an exclusive 9-hole international golf course, the project offers a premium lifestyle experience with low-density planning, open green landscapes, and high-end villas and residences.

It is conceptualized as a landmark luxury community where nature, privacy, and modern architecture come together to create a refined living environment.

Location Advantage

Located in Sector 10, Greater Noida West, ATS Kingston Heath enjoys excellent connectivity with key destinations of NCR while offering a peaceful and green environment.

Key highlights include:

  • Proximity to Pari Chowk and Noida-Greater Noida Expressway
  • Easy connectivity to Delhi and the Yamuna Expressway
  • Nearby metro stations are improving regional access
  • Access to major IT hubs and commercial zones
  • Surrounded by planned infrastructure and premium developments The location is well-connected while still maintaining a low-density, luxury residential character.
Apartments:

ATS Kingston Heath offers spacious and well-designed apartments in various configurations, including 2 BHK and 3 BHK. The apartments are designed to offer ample natural light and ventilation, and come with high-quality fixtures and fittings.

Investment Potential:

ATS Kingston Heath offers an excellent investment potential as the demand for quality housing in Noida is expected to rise in the coming years, leading to an increase in the value of properties in the area.

Environment-friendly features:

ATS Kingston Heath has been developed with a green building concept and includes environment-friendly features such as rainwater harvesting and sewage treatment plants.

About ATS Group 

The ATS Group is a diversified business conglomerate that operates in various sectors, including real estate, consumer goods, industrial engineering, appliances, and agriculture. Founded in 1897, the group has a presence in 80 countries and employs over 23,000 people worldwide. The group's commitment to innovation, sustainability, and social responsibility has earned it a reputation as one of India's most trusted and respected business groups.
